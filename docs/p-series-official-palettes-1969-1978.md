# Chevrolet P-Series and Step-Van official exterior-color audit, 1969-1978

This tranche records only what the reviewed Chevrolet data-book pages actually establish. Every cited page was rendered from the retained PDF and visually inspected. OCR was used only as a locator and transcription aid.

The result is deliberately uneven. The 1978 kit contains a closed Step-Van and Step-Van King exterior-color chart. The 1969-1971 kits preserve Union City body-finish options but omit the named Color and Trim charts. The 1972-1973 and 1975-1977 kits preserve only P30 motor-home chassis component finishes. The 1974 kit preserves FC Chassis and Step-Van component finishes but omits its referenced Exterior and Interior Color Selection Chart.

The normalized audit is `data/audits/p-series-official-palettes-1969-1978.json`.

## 1978 regular Step-Van palette

The official page N chart is titled “INTERIOR AND EXTERIOR COLOR AVAILABILITY CHART” and applies to “STEP-VAN AND STEP-VAN KING.” It states that two-tones are not available and that, for solid paint, the secondary color equals the primary color. `(M)` means metallic. The same page states that solid color paint `ZY1` is standard on steel `E32 Body Code` Step-Van models and available on aluminum `E33 Body Code` Step-Van models. All painted body areas receive the same color. Steel option sheets show ZY1 at no additional charge. Aluminum sheets price ZY1 separately. Special applications are handled by Union City Body Company, but the official pages do not name or code any such colors.

## Body-builder and chassis boundaries

For 1969-1971, Chevrolet's range pages state that the Step-Van chassis were shipped to Union City Body Co., where the body work was done. The listed `E30`, `E32`, `E33`, `E30AL`, `E32BM`, and `E33XH` identifiers are therefore recorded as body-finish option numbers, not paint-formula codes.

The 1969 P30 evidence covers gasoline and diesel steel and aluminum Step-Van King bodies. The 1970 printed-model-year evidence expands to P10, P20, and P30. The 1971 retained P-Series pages contract to P30 gasoline Step-Van King bodies. No named color is assigned merely because a solid or two-tone option existed.

The 1972-1973 and 1975-1977 evidence is explicitly Motor Home Chassis evidence. Black chassis paint, wheel paint, hub-cap combinations, and emblem trim are kept as component finishes. They are not promoted to Step-Van body colors.

The 1974 scan restores P10-P30 FC Chassis and steel/aluminum Step-Van scope. It records black FC chassis/components, white and black bumper distinctions, black mirrors and wheels, and steel-versus-aluminum grille, bezel, lettering, and molding finishes. Its body-color chart is referenced but absent.

## Specialty-color rule

The 1969-1971 and 1978 option pages acknowledge “Special applications” and route them to Union City Body Company for prices. None of the reviewed pages names Forest Service Green or supplies a Forest Service, USDA, or government-fleet paint code. Accordingly, this tranche makes no Forest Service availability assignment and does not bridge USDA references `14260` or `5032` into any Chevrolet model year.

## Explicit gaps

- No complete named P-Series or Step-Van exterior-color chart is retained for 1969-1977.
- The GM Heritage file named `1969-Chevrolet-G-Van.pdf` is not used as 1969 evidence. Its reviewed range pages explicitly say `1970 MODELS`.
- The 1975-1977 source pages carry prior-calendar-year publication dates. Their routing follows each official GM Heritage kit identity, and no page is carried into an adjacent year.
- No color name is reconstructed from photographs, brochures, adjacent years, USDA references, or generic Chevrolet palettes.
- The 1978 chart closes the regular solid-color program, not Union City special applications.
