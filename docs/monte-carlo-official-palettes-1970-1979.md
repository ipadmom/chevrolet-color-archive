# Chevrolet Monte Carlo official exterior palettes, 1970-1979

This tranche transcribes the model-specific exterior-color evidence in all ten GM Heritage Center Monte Carlo Vehicle Information Kits for 1970 through 1979. Every cited page was rendered and visually reviewed. OCR was used only to locate tables and assist transcription.

The normalized record is [monte-carlo-official-palettes-1970-1979.json](../data/audits/monte-carlo-official-palettes-1970-1979.json). It keeps regular body paint separate from two-tone combinations, vinyl roof materials, accent paint, stripes, and body-side moldings.

## Source-specific naming conflicts

The normalized record does not erase conflicts between official GM sections:

- 1973 code 60 is Yellow Orange Metallic on BODY-3 and Copper, Light (Metallic) in the price schedule.
- 1974 code 55 is Colonial Gold on BODY-3 and Sandstone in the sales chart.
- Engineering pages often use C/O and broad shade descriptions while price schedules reverse word order or use marketing names. Each primary row preserves its source_label_raw, and alternate official literals are recorded in restrictions.

These are source differences, not evidence for multiple same-code colors in the same year.

## Component and combination boundaries

- Two-tone rows preserve lower and upper order and the printed combination code. They are not duplicated as regular paint rows.
- Vinyl roof colors have component_role vinyl_roof_cover. Their compatibility and recommended or acceptable status remain restrictions.
- The 1975 and 1976 Landau pages identify stripe and molding components. Those components are not promoted to body paint.
- The 1976 Fashion-Tone page introduces accent code 16M Medium Gray Metallic. It remains an accent component.
- The 1979 D84 chart introduces Gray Metallic code 16 and Medium Blue Metallic code 85 as hood and roof accents. Neither is a regular single-color Monte Carlo body choice.
- The 1979 engineering chart states that the six D84 combinations are the only available combinations and permits no color overrides.

Interior upholstery colors were excluded even when they appeared beside exterior rows. Their presence was used only to identify the exterior table boundary and never as exterior availability evidence.

## Remaining gaps

- The official PDFs are pinned by hash, byte count, page count, and direct asset URL in the `brochure-source-archive-v1` GitHub Release.
- This model-specific audit does not establish or disprove Monte Carlo fleet, SEO, or special-order colors that are absent from these tables.
- Brochure swatch appearance is not treated as a machine-readable color value. The tranche preserves names, codes, roles, combinations, and restrictions, not sampled RGB approximations.
