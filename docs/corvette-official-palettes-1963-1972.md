# Chevrolet Corvette official exterior-color evidence, 1963-1972

This tranche transcribes the closed exterior-color tables in ten exact-year Chevrolet Corvette vehicle information kits retained from GM Heritage. Every cited PDF page was rendered from the retained artifact at 180 dpi and visually reviewed. OCR and extracted text were used only to locate candidate pages. No color was carried from an adjacent year, and interior trim, carpeting, belt, stripe, and upholstery colors were not promoted to body paint.

The normalized audit is [`data/audits/corvette-official-palettes-1963-1972.json`](../data/audits/corvette-official-palettes-1963-1972.json). It keeps regular body paint, convertible folding tops, and removable-hardtop vinyl covers in distinct programs.

## Source conflicts retained

The archive does not manufacture a reconciliation when two official tables disagree.

- 1969, code 990: PDF page 82 says Monaco Orange; PDF page 114 says Hugger Orange.
- 1969, code 988: PDF page 82 says Burgundy; PDF page 114 says Burgundy Maroon.
- 1972, code 98: PDF page 79 and PDF page 89 say Steel Cities Gray; PDF page 106 says Atlanta Gray.

The 1972 price schedule also limits the meaning of its trim-combination marks. Its Coupe combinations are recommendations, and non-recommended Coupe combinations may be submitted if the dealer initials the order-form block. The Convertible combinations shown are the only approved combinations. That rule is preserved as a source-scope limitation, not converted into additional paint colors.

## Remaining limits

- This tranche establishes the exact official color-table rows and component notes for 1963-1972 Corvette. It does not claim formula-level paint chemistry, supplier formulas, or midyear running changes not printed on the reviewed tables.
- Interior compatibility marks remain available in the source pages but are intentionally outside this body-paint transcription.
- No adjacent-year availability inference was used.
