# 2024 current Chevrolet model exterior-color source audit

## Result

Three retained GM-authored Fleet Guide revisions now supply visually reviewed
regular palette unions for all 16 current Chevrolet nameplates represented in
model year 2024. Seventeen table records beyond the previously reviewed
Suburban table add 145 exact source-color assertions and 128 distinct
model-year availability rows. The 2024 current-model total is 18 table records,
154 assertions, and 137 distinct model-year rows.

The source remains a qualified palette union. It prints color names and visible
footnote restrictions, but not factory paint codes. Each page says that
exterior colors vary by trim level and directs the reader to the governing
Order Guide. The archive therefore does not mark any of these model-years
complete.

## Newly resolved exact palette unions

The first retained 2025 guide revision prints this eight-color 2024 Equinox EV
union on PDF page 12: Black, Galaxy Gray Metallic, Iridescent Pearl Tricoat,
Radiant Red Tintcoat, Red Hot, Riptide Blue Metallic, Sterling Gray Metallic,
and Summit White.

It prints this eight-color 2024 Traverse union on PDF page 41: Harvest Bronze
Metallic, Iridescent Pearl Tricoat, Lakeshore Blue Metallic, Mosaic Black
Metallic, Radiant Red Tintcoat, Stardust Metallic, Sterling Gray Metallic, and
Summit White.

## Exact printed restrictions retained

- Page 12 of the first 2025 guide revision marks Equinox EV Iridescent Pearl
  Tricoat and Radiant Red Tintcoat as premium paint with an additional charge.
- Page 41 of the same revision applies the identical premium-paint restriction
  to Traverse Iridescent Pearl Tricoat and Radiant Red Tintcoat.
- Page 17 marks Blazer EV Iridescent Pearl Tricoat and Radiant Red Tintcoat
  as premium paint with an additional charge.
- Page 20 marks Silverado EV Black as a late model-year color.
- Page 32 marks five Corvette colors as premium paint: Accelerate Yellow
  Metallic, Amplify Orange Tintcoat, Rapid Blue, Red Mist Metallic Tintcoat,
  and Sea Wolf Gray Tricoat.
- Page 36 marks Trax Cacti Green as late model-year availability and marks
  Cayenne Orange Metallic, Fountain Blue, and Nitro Yellow Metallic as premium
  paint.
- Page 37 marks Trailblazer Fountain Blue, Iridescent Pearl Tricoat, and Nitro
  Yellow Metallic as premium paint.
- Pages 40, 47, 53, 61, 66, 70, 76, and 77 retain each visible premium-paint
  footnote at the individual color.
- The 2023 guide's 2024 Low Cab Forward page 77 lists Arc White, Cardinal Red,
  Dark Blue, Ebony Black, Wheatland Yellow, and Woodland Green for 3500 HG
  through 5500 XD. Page 78 lists Arc White only for 6500 XD and 7500 XD. That
  configuration boundary is retained on the five nonwhite colors.

## Resolved gaps and remaining qualification

The later 2024 guide's Traverse page 49 remains excluded because it is
explicitly labeled 2023. The first retained 2025 guide revision resolves that
gap with an explicitly labeled 2024 Traverse page. Its explicitly labeled 2024
Equinox EV page resolves the other current-nameplate gap. Page labels control,
not the cover year.

Both newly cited pages say that exterior colors vary by trim level and direct
the reader to the Order Guide. The governing 2024 U.S. Order Guide is not
retained. Exact trim restrictions remain unresolved, and neither page prints
factory paint or RPO codes. No code or unprinted restriction is inferred.

BrightDrop 400 and BrightDrop 600 begin in the Chevrolet catalog at model year
2025, so they are outside this 2024 denominator. No adjacent-year palette,
factory code, or specialty color is inferred.
