# 2025-2026 current Order Guide specialty tranche

## Result

Twenty-four retained GM Electronic Order Guide sources were visually reviewed
page by page. The tranche publishes 357 exact configuration-color rows:

- 12 year-specific rows for the six-color combined Tahoe Police Package and
  Special Service table.
- 12 Tahoe and Suburban retail/fleet rows, three colors for each exact source
  configuration and year.
- 108 Express rows, 18 colors for Cargo, Cutaway, and Passenger in each year.
- 186 Silverado HD rows, 21 colors for each of three light-HD configurations
  and 30 colors for the medium-duty configuration in each year.
- 31 Colorado WT SEO Paint rows, 17 for 2025 and 14 for 2026.
- Eight Blazer EV Police Package 9C1 and 9C3 SEO rows, four per year.

Every row is limited to the source heading, model year, color label, printed
SEO code, printed WA touch-up number, printed availability columns, and page
notes. No row establishes Forest Service Green. Isuzu Woodland Green `46U`
remains a separate Low Cab Forward identity with no printed WA number.

## Blazer EV Police Package

Both pages cover the listed 9C1 and 9C3 decor levels and print the same four
SEO identities: Victory Red `5T4 / WA-9260`, MSP Goose Blue
`9V2 / WA-5665`, Dark Blue Metallic `9V7 / WA-722J`, and Silver Ice Metallic
`9W5 / WA-636R`. These Order Guide pages do not establish 5W4 applicability.

The 2025 page marks all four rows `A`, but its row footnotes control the exact
availability record. Victory Red is noted as planned for Q1 2025 production.
MSP Goose Blue, Dark Blue Metallic, and Silver Ice Metallic are each footnoted
“Not available at this time.” The archive records those three rows as
footnoted unavailable at the reviewed revision, not simply available. The
2026 page contains no row-specific unavailability footnotes. Both pages warn
that SEO colors may require extended lead time.

## Completion boundary

Every exterior-color row on the 20 Tahoe, Suburban, Express, and Silverado HD
pages in this audit is now published for its exact source configuration and
year. The six standard Blazer EV police-program colors per year remain outside
the cited SEO tables and this specialty publication pass. The separate
Silverado 1500 retail/fleet pages still publish only their reviewed Woodland
Green rows.

The printed light-HD `5IS / WA-636R` value remains separate from the
`5IS / WA-363R` literal in the 2026 Silverado police and special-service
guides. `WA-722J` also remains three source-scoped identities: Tahoe
Dark Blue Metallic `9V7`, Express Dark Blue Metallic `5IH`, and Silverado
medium-duty Dark Ming Blue `5IH`.
